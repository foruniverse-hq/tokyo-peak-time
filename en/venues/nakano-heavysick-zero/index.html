What to expect here

Opened in 2002, it marked its 24th anniversary with multi-day events in September 2026. It is a hub for Japanese-language rap and the underground (THA BLUE HERB, 仙人掌), while RA also keeps listing techno/rave, UK hardcore and bass events. The focus is local, music-led events with entry at ¥1,500-2,500 and no pickup scene at all, with RA listings confirmed through September 2026.
