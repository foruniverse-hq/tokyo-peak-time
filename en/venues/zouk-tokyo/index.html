Run by Zouk Group and Belluna, this is a large venue built around VIP tables; guides describe an "open format" set list focused on getting the crowd going and "Vegas-style VIP", and it ranked 51st in DJ Mag's Top 100 Clubs 2026. RA lists a techno night in July 2025 and a one-off in October 2026, but no ongoing sound-focused programming. Operation was confirmed for August to October 2026.
