What to expect here

CIRCUS Tokyo has over 140 events a year on RA and is holding 11th anniversary events from October to December 2026 with underground acts such as Ben Sims, Makoto, Kasra, TSHA and Silva Bumpa; Time Out calls it one of Tokyo's most popular underground clubs. Its capacity is not disclosed on the official rental page or on technoairlines, and the figure of 1,000 on discotech is poorly sourced. It has two floors and is classed as mid-size, though it is borderline small.
