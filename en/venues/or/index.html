What to expect here

A cafe, gallery and music bar complex inside RAYARD MIYASHITA PARK (an R Lounge affiliate). Its official October 2026 schedule is mainstream-leaning lounge nights: late-night hip-hop such as ROULETTE TUESDAY, HE4VN. (anime songs and Vocaloid) and BOOGIE NIGHTS (disco), with no sign of techno/house sound-focused programming. As a cafe-bar it could be considered out of scope, but because it runs DJ nights it is classed as a pickup-oriented small venue.
